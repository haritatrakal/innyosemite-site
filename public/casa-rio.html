Overview

The first thing guests notice isn’t the view — it’s the sound of Lewis Creek flowing beside the property. Casa Rio sits on 9 private acres along the creek, with a hot tub, an ultimate game room (PS5, 85″ TV, ping pong, foosball, darts), and parking for 10+ vehicles. Sleeps 10 across four bedrooms, with the living room’s sofa beds stretching that to 12 when a group needs the extra room.
