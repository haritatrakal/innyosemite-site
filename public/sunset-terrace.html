Overview

Two private homes sharing one peaceful hilltop — a main house plus a separate guest house — built for families who want to stay together without stepping on each other. Spend the day in Yosemite or at Bass Lake, then come back for sunset dinners at the new 10-person outdoor dining area, a movie in the private theater, or a game of pickleball under the stars.
