Overview

A quiet wooded retreat built for movie nights, game nights, and everything in between. WoodUStay sleeps 14 across five bedrooms — two of them king suites — with a 98-inch smart TV movie room, a pool table and racing simulator, and a hot tub on the deck for winding down after a day at Yosemite or Bass Lake. Deer sightings are part of the scenery, and one small dog is welcome to come along.
